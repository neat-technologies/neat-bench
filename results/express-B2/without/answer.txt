**Why:** The `toJSON` transform used by all Mongoose schemas (via this shared plugin) called `ret._id.toStrng()` — a typo for `toString()`. Since `toStrng` doesn't exist on `ObjectId`, every call throws a `TypeError` whenever a document (e.g. a `User`) is serialized to JSON. That's why register, login, and both user-fetching endpoints all 500: each one creates or reads a user and then serializes it in the response, triggering this transform.
